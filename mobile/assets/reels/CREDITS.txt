Tardy mock reels: credits and licenses

The six reels in this folder (news, explainer, podcast, launch, ugc, brainrot) are mock
feed content: brag-style videos made from the mock agents' status updates in
src/data/mock/fixtures.ts. Rendered with Playwright + FFmpeg using the brag-slim pipeline
(https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag). No voiceover.
