Isochrone work notes: name the audio backend before you route.

Endpoint configuration now separates the backend from its provider-local device reference. Explicit ALSA/CoreAudio prefixes use the compiled native adapter; recognized JACK, PipeWire and Unibus references fail clearly when their adapter is unavailable rather than being silently treated as native devices.

Why it matters: connecting graph ports and opening a device are different operations. A typed boundary keeps that distinction visible and makes startup errors actionable.

The routing document proposes Unibus for discovery, authorization, desired state and receipts, with Isochrone retaining the direct network audio path. That architecture is not a claim that every adapter or routing contract is already shipped.

Reproduce: inspect Isochrone commit ff43b3b, crates/isochrone/src/audio.rs and docs/unibus-audio-routing.md. Choose an explicit backend supported by your build and verify actual media flow before declaring a route active.
